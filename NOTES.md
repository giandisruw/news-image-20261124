## Lead story

**The war spills over: NATO sends AWACS jets to Poland.** After
three Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles landed in Poland on Friday
20 November during a Russian air attack on Ukraine, NATO's North
Atlantic Council met in Brussels on Monday 23 November and
authorised the forward deployment of an AWACS early-warning aircraft
to Poland; allied fighters were already patrolling Polish skies.
A Russian drone also briefly violated Polish airspace during the
overnight attack, and President Zelenskiy warned on Monday that
Russian intelligence showed preparations for new strikes. The same
day, South Korea briefly issued an evacuation warning for Ulleung
island after a Russian aircraft entered its airspace. The front
page's main illustration — an AWACS E-3A Sentry over a map of
Poland and Eastern Europe — and its headline carry this story.
Reported 23–24 November 2026 (Reuters; NATO spokesperson Allison
Hart; Polish Defence Ministry).
